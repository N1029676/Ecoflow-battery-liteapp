EcoFlow Monitor

Getting started

  1. Close the EcoFlow phone app. The battery accepts only one Bluetooth connection at a time.
  2. Use Chrome or Edge (desktop or Android) on an HTTPS page. Safari and iOS browsers do not support Web Bluetooth.
  3. Open Advanced and enter your EcoFlow user ID once (there is a lookup form there). The battery must already be linked to your account in the EcoFlow app.
  4. Press Connect and choose your battery (it appears as ECOFLOW_BLE). Stay close; a weak signal can make connecting take a few tries.

Good to know

  • Tap a tile to see more detail.
  • Readings are decoded from community protocol notes; some units are not independently verified.
  • History is recorded on this device only, and only while the page is open and connected. Nothing is uploaded.
  • The page reconnects on its own if the link drops. Browsers pause pages in the background, so keep it in the foreground to record.
  • Over HTTPS you can install it as an app (Install app or your browser menu) and it opens offline.
Web Bluetooth is not available here. Use Chrome or Edge on desktop or Android, served over HTTPS or from localhost.
